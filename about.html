ABOUT TAP FOR HELP

One tap. One clear next step.

TapForHelp is an independent community resource project created to make the first connection to help easier.

Why I started TapForHelp

Sometimes help exists, but people never find it. People may miss benefits, treatment, assistance, or services simply because nobody connected them with the right resource.

Finding help often requires knowing the right program, agency, or search term before a person can even begin. I created TapForHelp to make that first connection easier.

Brian Marino, Founder

What TapForHelp does

  • Helps people discover public, nonprofit, government, and community resources
  • Uses plain language to help people choose a useful next step
  • Lets visitors contact resource providers directly

What TapForHelp is not

TapForHelp is not a healthcare provider, mental health provider, law firm, government agency, benefits administrator, emergency-response service, crisis hotline, or official representative of 988.
